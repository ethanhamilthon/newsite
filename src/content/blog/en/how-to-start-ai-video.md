Most people look for the best AI video tool. In practice, experience matters more than the tool.

## 1. Pick one tool

Do not test ten tools at once. Pick one and learn it deeply. For me it is Google Flow.

## 2. Learn to write prompts

A good prompt describes the shot precisely: character, place, light, camera movement.

## 3. Make something every day

One small video a day. In a month you will not recognize your own results.
